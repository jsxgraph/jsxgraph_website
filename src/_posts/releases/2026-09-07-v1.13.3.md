Dear friends of JSXGraph,

JSXGraph v1.13.3 is a patch release that primarily fixes a regression introduced in v1.13.0 affecting the strokeColor of polygon elements.

This release also includes contributions from @cdeil, who added missing 3D TypeScript declarations, fixed ID collisions in anonymous containers, and improved the Makefile for macOS. Additionally, @anupamme addressed several minor security issues. Many thanks.

For a complete list of changes, please refer to the CHANGELOG.

Enjoy, Alfred
