Dear friends of JSXGraph,

we are pleased to announce the __7th International JSXGraph Conference__, taking place __online from 6–8 October 2026__.

__Conference website__: <https://jsxgraph.org/conf2026/>

The conference brings together developers, educators, instructional designers, and researchers who are interested in using __JSXGraph__ to enrich the digital teaching and learning of STEM subjects. Whether you are an experienced JSXGraph user or are just beginning to explore its possibilities, the conference offers an opportunity to exchange ideas, share experiences, and learn from the community—regardless of your location.

__The conference will be held entirely online__, enabling participants from around the world to attend and contribute without the need for travel. Participation is free of charge; however, all attendees are kindly requested to register.

We warmly invite contributions in the form of talks or workshops. Possible topics include:

- concepts, experiences, and workflows involving JSXGraph,
- applications, projects, and best practices,
- pedagogical approaches and teaching concepts,
- JSXGraph integrations and plug-ins,
- extensions and plug-ins developed for JSXGraph.

__Deadline for submission of talks and workshop proposals:
September 18th, 2026__

Further information about the online conference platform and technical details will be announced in due course.

The __7th International JSXGraph Conference 2026__ is organized by the [Center of Mobile Learning with Digital Technology](https://mobile-learning.uni-bayreuth.de/) at the [University of Bayreuth](https://uni-bayreuth.de).

We look forward to welcoming you online in October 2026.

With best regards,

Carsten Miller and Alfred Wassermann
