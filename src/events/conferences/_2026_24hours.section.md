# 24 Hours of JSXGraph (On-Site in Bayreuth)

## 4th – 5th March 2026

The second in-person “24 hours of JSXGraph” meeting took place at the University of Bayreuth. The event was a great success.

The meeting ran from noon to noon, allowing participants from the German-speaking region to attend with at most two overnight stays. As a result, most participants came from this region and German was used as the conference language.

[24 Hours of JSXGraph 2026](https://jsxgraph.org/24hours2026/)

On Thursday afternoon, the event was preceded by the first in-person meeting of the SASIM group (STACK task collection for engineering mathematics).

The meeting was organized by the Center of Mobile Learning with Digital Technology at the University of Bayreuth, Germany.
