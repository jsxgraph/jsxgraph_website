# 24 Hours of JSXGraph (On-Site in Bayreuth)

## 24th – 25th February 2027

Join us in Bayreuth for "24 Hours of JSXGraph, February 24–25, 2027"! This informal on-site meeting brings together JSXGraph experts, educators, researchers, and developers to share ideas, explore applications, present projects, and discuss the future of interactive mathematics. We look forward to connecting, learning, and creating together.
