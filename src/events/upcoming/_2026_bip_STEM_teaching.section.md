# Supporting STEM Teaching through Digital Assessment Practices (BIP)

## 5th – 9th October 2026, Tallinn (Estonia)

The event explores AI-aware digital assessment practices in STEM education, with a particular focus on integrating JSXGraph into STACK and Moodle to create interactive, visually rich assessment tasks that enhance mathematical teaching and learning.  

Rathmann, Wigand (Educator), Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Germany/University of Bayreuth, Germany
